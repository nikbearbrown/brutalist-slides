# Logos for this style

Put the official logo files here and name them in `../style.json` under `logos` (`mark`, `mark-on-dark`, `lockup`, `lockup-on-dark`). SVG is best.

Files in this folder are not committed to the repository: logos are trademarks of their owners. Say here where the official files are downloaded and who may use them.

If the folder is empty the logo slot on the title and closing slides stays empty. Nothing else changes.
